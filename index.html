About Lawtte

We're your neighbours. We make your coffee.

We are Lawtte. A student-led coffee setup in Puncak Perdana built specifically for those who refuse to slow down. We don't believe in corporate gimmicks, overcomplicated menus, or overpriced drinks.

Whether you're a student pulling a 2 AM all-nighter or a remote worker locking into an early morning sprint, we serve premium espresso, high-grade matcha, and guilt-free 0-sugar options to keep you sharp without the crash.

No compromise, no fluff — just real fuel delivered straight to your block or ready at our stall in minutes.
